# Two exceptional Hodge classes of Fermat varieties have length exactly seven

Miranda, Movasati, Rufino and Villaflor ([arXiv:2609.27301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27301)) prove the Hodge conjecture
for the Fermat varieties of degree below 65, except 44, 51 and 52. Their method bounds by six the *length* of an exceptional
class; in degrees 44 and 52 their tables give the upper bound 7. This project proves in Lean 4 that it is exactly 7 for
the two representatives below.

| Theorem | Statement |
|---|---|
| `FermatLength.lenMod_xi44` | in degree 44, `ξ = (8, 11, 15, 19, 27, 31, 43)` has length 7 modulo `D_44 + S_44` |
| `FermatLength.lenMod_xi52` | in degree 52, `ξ = (1, 5, 9, 33, 37, 48, 49)` has length 7 modulo `D_52 + S_52` |
| `FermatLength.lenMod_xi44_all`, `FermatLength.lenMod_xi52_all` | the same modulo a larger subgroup, with more of Aoki's standard tuples |

All use only the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; there is no `native_decide`.

## Definitions

`R_d` is the free abelian group on the nonzero residues modulo `d`, and the length of an element is the sum of the absolute values
of its coefficients. `D_d` is generated by the pairs `(a) + (-a)` and by `(d/2)`, and `S_d` by Aoki's standard tuples. The length of
a class is the least length of an element that differs from it by an element of `D_d + S_d`.

## The proof

Two kinds of additive functions on `R_d` vanish on every generator: for each unit `t`, the sum of `2 (t a mod d) - d` (Shioda's
condition), and the parity of the number of residues of order 11 (degree 44) or 13 (degree 52). On `ξ` the first are zero and the
parity is odd. An element of length at most six can be replaced by a list of at most six residues with the same values, and an
exhaustive check shows that there is none. The soundness of the check is proved, and the kernel evaluates it.

## What it does not do

It does not prove or disprove the Hodge conjecture for any variety. It says that the criterion "length at most six" cannot be met in
degrees 44 and 52 without lifting the class to a larger degree, which is the other method of the paper and is not treated here. Nor
is degree 51.

The first representative is the one printed in Table 2 of the paper. The paper prints none in degree 52; that the second one lies in
the exceptional class was checked by computer outside Lean, and the theorems do not depend on it.
